# Cabling workstream status

## Done

- `Ground.Cabling.generatedFusion` constructs the genuine braided fusion
  subcategory generated by any object. `generatedFusionInclusion` is full,
  faithful, monoidal and braided; `generatedFusionInclusion_essentialImage`
  identifies its essential image, and `generatedFusionInclusionBraidImageIso`
  identifies the corresponding braid images.
- `generatedCategoryAbelian`, `generatedCategoryRigid`, and
  `generatedBy_rightDual` construct the inherited structure without auxiliary
  hypotheses. `unit_retract_power` proves that a positive power of each simple
  object contains the unit, and finite semisimplicity handles arbitrary
  generators.
- `Ground.simple_strand_inclusion`, combining fusion's split-unit theorem with
  `strandEmbedding`, gives injections from smaller to larger braid images for
  every simple object.
- `strandPermutation`, `pureWords`, and `pureBraidImage_finiteIndex` construct
  the pure-word image and prove its finite index. `braidImageRetractMap` is a
  surjective map from the braid image of an object to that of any retract.
- `PowerExpr.isCable_braiding` derives actual braid-image membership for
  braidings of arbitrary tensor expressions from the hexagons. The tuple
  normalization comparison and block component argument give
  `powerSum_pureBlock_mem` for all pure words and all tuple blocks.
- `objectPropertyF_of_generated` proves property F passes from an object to
  every object it generates. `Ground.Cabling.generatedFusion_hasPropertyF`
  proves the bundled generated category inherits property F.
- `cablingWitness_generated` constructs the quotient-of-a-subgroup of a
  product of cabled braid images, with finite-index pure image, for any
  generated object.
- Fusion's `simple_fpDimension_sq_integer_of_weaklyIntegral` proves ENO 8.27
  by a Galois argument. `generated_simple_square_integral` applies it to the
  generated fusion category and the ambient simple generator; FP dimension
  preservation is `generatedFusionInclusion_fpDimension`.
- `generatedInputsOfGround` constructs every legacy `GeneratedInputs` field
  for the compatibility bundle of any genuine ground braided fusion category.
  This bridge intentionally mentions the legacy interface and is not used by
  the bundle-free ground theorems.
