# Conditional formalization and assumption ledger

Theorem 1.1 is stated and proved as
`PropertyF.propertyF_implies_weaklyIntegral` in `PropertyF/Main.lean`. Its
hypotheses include the paper's **pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate** condition,
property F on the original category, and an explicit `StandardInputs` bundle.
The proof includes the reduction to the conjugate, not merely the
pseudo-unitary special case.

**This is a conditional formalization.** The numerical, matrix, braid-algebra,
centralizer and contradiction arguments are kernel checked. The standard-input
bundle also contains categorical realization bridges that have **not** been
constructed from Mathlib's category structures. In particular, this repository
does not yet provide a fully constructed spherical/fusion/modular category
library or a construction of `StandardInputs`. Those limitations are visible
hypotheses, not discharged by the absence of `sorry`.

The complete six-page paper was read before implementation. `PLAN.md` gives
its exhaustive definition/result map and the audit of the pinned Mathlib source.

## What is defined concretely

`PropertyF/Definitions.lean` uses Mathlib categories, monoidal and braided
structures, rigidity, complex linearity, abelian categories, simple objects,
biproducts, retracts, and finite-dimensional Hom spaces.

- `FusionData` specifies finitely many pairwise nonisomorphic simples, the
  simple unit, and a finite direct-sum decomposition of every object.
- `fpSimple` is a positive normalized **fusion-ring character**, satisfying
  the fusion equations with actual Hom-space dimensions as coefficients. This
  character specification of FP dimension uses [ENO §8]. Existence and
  uniqueness of the positive character are not reproved.
- Object FP dimensions are finite sums of the character against actual simple
  Hom multiplicities. Global FP dimension is the sum of its squares.
- Intrinsic global dimension is a sum of squared norms computed by actual
  evaluation and coevaluation composites. `GlobalDimensionData` supplies a
  genuine ℂ-algebra equivalence `End(1) ≃ₐ[ℂ] ℂ` and simple double-dual
  isomorphisms. Independence of these choices is standard [ENO §2.1], not
  reproved here. No arbitrary global-dimension constant is used.
- Tensor powers and adjacent braid generators include explicit associators.
  Braid images are the **subgroups generated by these automorphisms**. This is
  a direct definition of the image; an Artin-group presentation and its
  representation homomorphism are not separately implemented.
- `braidImage X n` means **n+2 strands**. `braidImageAll` includes the trivial
  zero- and one-strand groups for cabling tensor-unit factors.
- Property F means finiteness of all these groups for every object. Weak
  integrality means the global FP dimension equals an integer. Pseudo-unitarity
  means equality of intrinsic global dimension and global FP dimension.
- Galois conjugacy is an actual braided monoidal equivalence with semilinear
  action on morphisms for a specified field automorphism. All complex field
  automorphisms fix ℚ and restrict to Qbar. Normal-field automorphisms are
  extended to ℂ in the explicit arithmetic input.
- `GeneratedBy X Y` means that Y is a retract of a finite sum of nonnegative
  tensor powers of X. Identification with the generated rigid fusion
  subcategory is standard [EGNO 4.11.4], represented by `GeneratedInputs`.

The Drinfeld centre and its half-braidings are Mathlib's existing construction,
not an opaque replacement. The centre's fusion and modular structure is an
explicit standard input.

## Assumption ledger

**Zero `sorry` proofs. Zero global custom `axiom` declarations.** All unproved
literature and construction inputs used by the proofs are structure fields in
`PropertyF/Axioms.lean`. Structure fields remain hypotheses even when
`#print axioms` lists only the usual logical foundations.

The following tables cover the proof-bearing fields, as well as the data whose
existence is assumed. The source file contains each exact dependent Lean type.

Thus the **remaining work** for a fully constructed category-level development
is substantial: spherical trace/pivotal infrastructure, finite-group averaging
and compatible block decompositions, coherence maps for braid-image transport,
the dominant centre product diagram, and categorical pure-braid cabling.
These are not hidden `sorry`s, nor should they be mistaken for completed Lean
constructions merely because they are conventional mathematics.

## Paper audit

No mathematical error or irreparable gap in the six-page argument was
established. The following places require explicit conventions or structural
facts in a formal development:

1. **Page 3, Lemma 3.1, partial-trace paragraph.** Associators, which side is
   traced, bimodularity, nonzero `d`, and the simple-strand scalar trace must
   be supplied. These are explicit fields of `OverlapModel`; the product and
   recurrence are then proved.
2. **Page 4, Lemma 3.2, equality chain.** The weights must all be nonzero to
   force scalar action on every retained block. The paper proves this;
   `weight_ne_zero` reproduces the argument. Zero-dimensional matrices are
   handled separately by the matrix equality theorem.
3. **Page 5, main proof, displayed square root.** Arbitrary Galois automorphisms
   are not order preserving. The argument is valid because positivity first
   identifies `f * τ(δ) = δ`; one then transports this algebraic identity.
   `positive_dimension_ratio` proves this from the squared relation without
   asserting that Galois maps commute with the positive real square-root
   function.
4. **Page 6, Lemma 4.2, `X=0` and positive powers.** The paper separates the
   zero case. The definition here permits exponent zero and trivial zero-/one-
   strand groups, so the conditional cabling proof handles all X uniformly.

The cited [EGNO 4.11.4], [EGNO 8.20.14], and [EGNO 9.3.4] were checked against
the [author's final book PDF](https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/egnobookfinal.pdf).
The first is the claimed rigidity result; the latter two give centre modularity
and the squared categorical global dimension. No citation correction is needed.
